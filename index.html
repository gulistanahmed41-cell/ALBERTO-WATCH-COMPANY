INSIDE THE TIMEPIECE

The technology behind
every second.

Different watches use different technologies to keep time. Understanding the basics helps you choose a timepiece that suits your routine, preferences and approach to watch care.

01

Mechanical Movement

A mechanical watch uses a system of springs and gears to measure time. Many enthusiasts appreciate the traditional engineering and craftsmanship involved.

02

Quartz Precision

Quartz watches use an electronic oscillator regulated by a quartz crystal. They are widely valued for convenience, accuracy and straightforward everyday use.

03

Solar Technology

Solar-powered watches convert light into energy to power the movement. The exact charging and maintenance needs depend on the model and manufacturer.
